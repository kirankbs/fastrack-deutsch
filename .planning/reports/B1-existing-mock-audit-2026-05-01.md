## Top 5 cross-mock issues

1. **Sprachbausteine register monoculture — all 20 SB texts are informal "Liebe/Lieber X" private letters with `du`-form.** No formal register (`Sehr geehrte`/`Sie`) appears in any SB cloze. Real telc B1 mixes registers; learners coached only on `du`-form will be blindsided by formal SB texts on test day. Highest-priority fix: rewrite roughly half of SB1 or SB2 across the 10 mocks into formal register (e.g. complaint to landlord, application follow-up, formal apology to supervisor) with full `Sie`-form.

2. **All 10 writing prompts are letters/E-Mails, with addressees mostly known/semi-known** (Sprachschule, Chef, VHS-Kursleiter, Kundenservice, Reiseveranstalter, Hausarztpraxis). No fully formal Beschwerde-an-Behörde, no Bewerbungsanschreiben, no Leserbrief. Mocks 04 and 10 come closest to formal complaints but still read as semi-formal. The bank should include 2–3 fully formal variants.

3. **Listening Teil 3 question stems are templated identically across all 10 mocks.** L3-Q2 is "Worauf einigen sich die beiden [Freund(inn)e]/Kolleg(inn)en am Ende?" 10 times out of 10. L3-Q3 starts "Was empfiehlt der/die …" 10/10. L3-Q5 is "Was ist das größte Problem …" in 6/10. Repeat-takers will pattern-match the question structure rather than the audio.

4. **SP3 planning collapses to ~3 archetypes**: party/festival planning (mocks 01, 03, 06, 07, 10), corporate "Tag organisieren" (05, 08), joint-trip planning (09). Worse, the **task verbs** are nearly always "organisieren" + "einigen Sie sich" with the same 6-bullet structure. SP1 introduce prompt is byte-identical across all 10 mocks (canonical-OK, but worth noting).

5. **Theme clustering and lexical re-use between mocks**: mock 04 (Bildschirmzeit) overlaps mock 01 R2; mock 05 Umwelt R3 ads (Reparatur-Café, E-Auto, Solar) overlap mock 10 Konsum R3 (Reparatur-Werkstatt, Second-Hand-Möbel); mock 06 Gesellschaft (Ehrenamt) overlaps mock 07 Kultur (ehrenamtliches Straßenfest) and mock 03 SP3 (Abschlussfeier). Reading Teil 3 ad clusters reuse the same lexical fields (Reparatur, Second-Hand, Mitfahrgelegenheit) across 4+ mocks. Consider shifting mock 10 toward Verbraucherrecht/Garantie to differentiate from mock 05.
